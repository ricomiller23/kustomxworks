# Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) Campaign Implementation Guide

**Brand**: KustomXworks Handyman & Construction Services  
**Objective**: Leads / Conversions (`quote_request`)  
**Domain**: https://kustomxworks.com  
**Event Optimization**: Lead Form Submission (`quote_request`)

---

## 1. Campaign Structure & Budgeting

| Campaign Setting | Configuration | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Buying Type** | Auction | Standard |
| **Campaign Objective** | Leads | Optimized for conversion event `quote_request` |
| **Bidding Strategy** | Lowest Cost (Auto-bid); switch to Cost-Per-Lead (CPL) cap (~$60–$75) after 20 conversions | Prevents high initial acquisition costs |
| **Placements** | Advantage+ Placements | Automated delivery across FB/IG Feeds, Stories, Reels |

### Budget Scenarios & Allocation Splits
- **Lean**: $800/month (~$27/day)
- **Standard**: $1,500/month (~$50/day)
- **Scale**: $2,500/month (~$83/day)
- **Budget Distribution**:
  - 70% Cold Acquisition (Ad Set 1)
  - 20% Website Retargeting (Ad Set 3)
  - 10% Creative Testing / Lookalikes (Ad Set 2)

## 2. Ad Sets & Audience Targeting

### Ad Set 1: Inland Empire Homeowners (Cold Prospecting)
- **Locations**: Corona, Murrieta, Temecula, Perris, Moreno Valley, Norco, Hemet, Beaumont, Palm Springs (+20-mile radius).
- **Age**: 35 – 65+
- **Gender**: All (Homeowners)
- **Detailed Targeting**:
  - Homeownership status: Homeowner
  - Interests: *Home improvement, Landscaping, Home repair, General contractor, DIY/Interior design, Real estate, Property management*

### Ad Set 2: Lookalike 1%–3% (Week 4+)
- **Source**: Custom Audience of Website Lead Submissions + Customer List (CSV export from `/leads`).
- **Target**: 1% – 2% Lookalike Audience in California (Riverside & San Bernardino Counties).

### Ad Set 3: Full Funnel Retargeting (Week 2+)
- **Audience**:
  - Website Visitors (Past 60 days) excluding converted leads.
  - Page views on `/pricing`, `/book`, and `/services`.
  - Social Engagers (FB & IG page interaction past 90 days).
