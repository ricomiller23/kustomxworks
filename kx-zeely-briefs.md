## 4. Zeely Campaign Guardrails & Compliance
1. **No License Misrepresentations**: Do not claim "State Licensed General Contractor" on ad copy. KustomXworks operates under the California B&P § 7048 Handyman Exemption for minor work under $500, partnering with licensed contractors for structural permits.
2. **Speed-to-Lead Guarantee**: All ads emphasize "Same-Week Flat-Rate Estimates" or "Same-Day Response".
3. **Transparent Pricing**: Avoid "Cheap" or "Lowest Price" hooks. Use "Honest Pricing", "Flat-Rate Quotes", and "No Hidden Fees".
