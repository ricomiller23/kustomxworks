## 3. Funnel Conversion Benchmarks

To achieve the projected returns, the campaign relies on our high-converting, speed-to-lead website funnel:

1. **Ad Click to Landing Page**:
   - Google Search CPC: ~$4.50 – $6.50
   - Meta/Zeely CPC: ~$1.20 – $2.10
2. **Landing Page to Form Completion**:
   - Target conversion rate: **8% – 12%** (fueled by service preselection at `/book?service=` and mobile click-to-call buttons).
3. **Speed-to-Lead Response**:
   - Within 5 minutes: **4x higher close rate**.
   - Autoresponder confirms receipt immediately; hot leads alert owner on phone.
4. **Lead to Estimate Scheduled**:
   - Target benchmark: **45% – 55%** of valid inquiries.
5. **Estimate to Closed Won**:
   - Target close rate: **35% – 45%** when backed by upfront flat-rate transparency.

## 4. Cash Flow & Optimization Timeline

- **Weeks 1–2 (Calibration Phase)**:
  - Run Google Ads on Maximize Clicks with $7.00 bid ceiling.
  - Run Meta on Lowest Cost to gather 20+ pixel conversion events.
  - Filter out search terms containing negative keywords.
- **Weeks 3–4 (Optimization Phase)**:
  - Transition Google Ads to Maximize Conversions (tCPA target $70–$90).
  - Launch Meta Retargeting on website visitors.
  - Export CSV from `/leads` to identify which city generates the highest margin.
- **Weeks 5–8 (Scaling Phase)**:
  - Allocate 65% of budget to top 2 performing services (typically Concrete Block Walls & Artificial Turf).
  - Activate Lookalike 1% audience in Meta using confirmed customer emails.
